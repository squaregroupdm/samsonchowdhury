Founder, Square Group 1925 - 2012

Samson HChowdhury

A village pharmacy in Pabna became one of Bangladesh's largest conglomerates. Remembered here, in his words and theirs.

Read his story News Room
The man

To his followers he was a hero. To his contemporaries, an icon. To young entrepreneurs, a mentor. To regulators, a symbol of fairness. He put people's welfare at the centre of enterprise, and in five decades there was never a single day of labour unrest in any company he built.

Eighty-six years, one line

25 September1925

Born at Aruakandi, Gopalganj

Eldest son of Eakub Hussain Chowdhury, a mission-hospital medical officer, and Latika Chowdhury.

Age 171943

Joins the Royal Indian Navy

Leaves home without telling his family. Refuses a signals posting, spends five days in custody, and gets the radar unit he wanted.

February1946

Joins the naval mutiny

Rises against British rule with his shipmates. Walks away with a clean discharge and a recommendation for government service.

6 August1947

Marries Anita Biswas

He is 22. She will be his only assistant in his first factory, and his companion for 64 years.

Ataikula, Pabna1952

Takes over Hossain Pharmacy

Quits the post office and returns home to run his father's medicine shop.

Esons1956

Borrows Tk 5,000 to make syrups

Owner, worker, distributor and salesman in one, manufacturing at home.

Rs 17,0001958

Square is founded

Four friends, equal partners, a rented tin shed in Pabna and twelve workers. No profit for three years.

Janssen1974

Licence from Janssen Pharmaceutica

A Johnson and Johnson subsidiary. The plant is rebuilt to international standards.

Bangladesh1985

Market leader

Ahead of every national and multinational company. Still is.

Export1987

First Bangladeshi pharmaceutical exporter

Square becomes the first company in the country to sell medicine abroad.

IPO1991

Square goes public

Investors pay Tk 900 for shares with a Tk 100 face value.

AmCham1998

Business Executive of the Year

For professional excellence, high ethical standards and contribution to society.

Dhaka2006

Square Hospitals opens

Pharmaceuticals, hospitals, textiles, toiletries, consumer goods, agro-vet, IT and television.

NBR2008

Highest taxpayer in the country

Honoured on the first National Income Tax Day.

5 January2012

Passes away in Singapore

Laid to rest at Astra Farmhouse, Pabna. The cabinet adopts a condolence motion.

Posthumous2013

Ekushey Padak

One of the highest civilian honours of Bangladesh, in the Social Welfare category.
